03 / Method

Reading the evidence

Scope before scores

01

Start with the source

Follow the exact paper, repository, or project link. Check its date, version, protocol, and what the claim actually covers.

02

Check the setting

A simulated drive, a physical road test, and a clinical case answer different questions. Medical cases do not establish patient outcomes; simulations do not establish real-world performance.

03

Keep limits attached

Read the limitations and next verification step alongside each entry. A missing score is unknown, not zero. A live source is not a guarantee of current validity.

No custom or aggregate ratings are assigned.

What the source statuses mean +
Historical
Evidence describes a past test or release; present availability still needs checking.
Live
The catalog’s cited source documented availability at its recorded research date. This interface does not poll it live.
Ongoing
The cited work was in progress; its protocol or findings may change.
Unverified
The source identity, availability, or claim needs confirmation before use.
