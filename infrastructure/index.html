Reading the atlas

Useful detail. Visible uncertainty.

A selected sample. This is representative coverage, not a census or market-share ranking. Absence from the map does not mean a company has no activity there.

Capacity has a meaning. Operating and proposed projects stay distinct. IT load, grid supply, facility power, wafer capacity, and capex are different measures. No global GW total is calculated.

Relationships have a scope. Public company partnerships do not establish a specific fab, memory supplier, or deployment. Unknown assignments remain explicit.
