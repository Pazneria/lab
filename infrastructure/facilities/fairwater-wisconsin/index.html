Building 2 is operational. This is estimated based on (1) the delay between Building 1 and Building 2 starting land clearing, (2) in March 2025, a Microsoft spokesperson [said](https://www.wpr.org/news/microsoft-pauses-construction-on-parts-of-mount-pleasant-site-again) “fully on track” with the first phase , where “southern datacenter buildings complete, and preparation begins for operations” was [expected](https://local.microsoft.com/blog/mount-pleasant-datacenter-project-update/) by late 2026. Finally, while it's not 100% consistent with our estimates, MISO was [planning](https://cdn.misoenergy.org/NEW%20LOAD%20ANNOUNCEMENTS%20IN%20MISO%20REGIONS%2012062024684954.pdf) 1480 MW of load for Microsoft in Mount Pleasant in 2026.
