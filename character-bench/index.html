How this comparison works

Only two distinct, verified attempts of the exact same prompt can be paired. A/B order is randomized. Each character is centered and fitted to the same unit bounding sphere without rewriting its frozen GLB; this compares framing rather than physical size.

The original PBR materials are preserved. Clay and wireframe are optional inspection modes. White studio lights, exposure, environment and the movable key light are shared across both views. Submitted lights and animations do not run. A rig is optional.

Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom. With a view focused, arrow keys rotate, +/− zoom, and Home resets. Each view also has Front, Back and Close-up controls. Model labels remain hidden until both files load and you choose a preference. Choices reset when you change pairs or leave the page.

Frozen asset hashes are verified before import. A failed import stays outside voting. Source timings and infrastructure limits are shown after your preference without a quality ranking.
