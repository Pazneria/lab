FALLOWMERE STATION
North Weald Railway

Launch
Run "python launch.py" in this folder. Open the loopback address printed in the terminal. The preferred address is http://127.0.0.1:43871/. If that port is occupied, the launcher requests a free port. Ctrl+C stops the server. All scene assets are included locally.

Controls
Choose Begin walk. Move with W, A, S and D. Look with the mouse. Hold Shift for a brisk walk. Esc pauses and releases the mouse. R or Reset returns to the entrance. The arrow keys move forward/back and turn left/right. Drag to look when mouse capture is unavailable. On a touch device, use the left pad to move and drag the view to look.

Walk
Go through the open gate and climb the steps into the waiting room. Its second door leads to the canopy. Turn right and follow the platform ramp down to the plank crossing. Beyond the crossing, the path bends left through Ash Wood. It passes the clearing overlooking the station and loops back by the old service crossing.

Requires a current browser with WebGL 2 and DecompressionStream support. The time of day and cast shadows are fixed. The walkable area covers the station and woodland circuit. This build was packaged and checked on the CPU. Visual appearance, browser interaction and frame timing are left for the inspection session.
