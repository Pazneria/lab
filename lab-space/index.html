Lab / Research room

Design references

Visible apparatus and work surfaces from Faraday's laboratory at the Royal Institution; daylight and open circulation from Salk's architecture; quiet instrument work from Bell Labs' historical photographs. An original room, with no institutional affiliation. The instruments are decorative; the benchmark bench opens the public catalog. The north-wall screen compares walkable worlds.

Research room

Move around

Click or tap the floor to walk there. Select an instrument or piece of furniture to approach it. Select the benchmark bench or home door to walk over and open its controls. Drag to look.

The north-wall screen has two static scene previews. Click a preview to enter it; use the screen arrows for another pair. Exit scene returns to the same room position and pair. Compare worlds opens accessible controls; the standalone Walkable 3D page includes full build inspectors and personal grades.

Focus the room to walk with WASD. Arrow keys walk and turn. Enter or E opens a nearby station. Manual movement or dragging stops a route; a new tap replaces it. Esc stops walking and releases room focus.

Direction buttons support holds and small taps. The catalog and home links are always available above the room.
