Explore the Lab

Click the room or choose Explore to capture the mouse. Escape opens these controls. Close them to keep walking, or choose Return to room to capture again.

Use WASD or arrow keys to walk, Shift to walk faster, C to crouch, and R to return to the entrance. Press E to use the station you are looking at. Enter captures an uncaptured mouse; after capture, it can also use a station.

Select Ivo inside the camera ring to enter CharacterBench, where two attempts of the same character prompt are compared side by side. SceneBench is on the north wall: select a preview to enter a scene, then return to the same comparison. Open both scenes before voting; the leaderboard and model reveal are available in Compare scenes.

Walk south through both sliding door sets to return home. The Lab menu and Walk to exit shortcut also provide direct exit access.

Lab navigation stays available in the menu. Ivo is a completed Sol character build shown as an exhibit; its display is not a benchmark result.
