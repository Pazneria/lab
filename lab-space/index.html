Explore the Lab

Click the room or choose Explore to capture the mouse. Escape opens these controls. Close them to keep walking, or choose Return to room to capture again.

Use WASD or arrow keys to walk, Shift to walk faster, C to crouch, and R to return to the entrance. Press E to use the station you are looking at. Enter captures an uncaptured mouse; after capture, it can also use a station.

Select Ivo inside the camera ring to enter CharacterBench, where two attempts of the same character prompt are compared side by side. SceneBench takes you to the lower Compare Worlds screen in the west alcove, on your left from the entrance. Aim at a scene card and click or press E to inspect it full-screen. Exit the scene to return in front of the same screen, then inspect the other scene and vote on the display. Next chooses a new comparison. Accessible controls is an optional keyboard and screen-reader panel with model reveal and the leaderboard.

Walk south through both sliding door sets to return home. The Lab menu and Exit / Home shortcut also provide direct exit access.

Lab navigation stays available in the menu. Ivo is a completed Sol character build shown as an exhibit; its display is not a benchmark result.
