Cost has a scope

RuneBench uses an API-equivalent estimate for OAuth runs. BullshitBench reports covered candidate-response telemetry. Neither is a complete benchmark bill. Missing medical costs stay unknown.

02

Time has a clock

RuneBench curves follow gameplay tracker time. BullshitBench measures instrumented requests including network waits and retries. Neither measures pure model inference speed.

03

Uncertainty stays visible

These slices have no reported repeat-trial intervals. Differences are descriptive; they do not establish statistical significance or a causal effect of reasoning effort.
