# Benchmark source review, November 23, 2026

This refresh records public source results and does not run evaluations. The AA v5.0.1 server-rendered selection includes GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol and Gemini 4 Neon; it is not the complete leaderboard. Arena's accessible November 22 source view supplies a cost-frontier subset, not the full ranking, and does not expose individual vote counts or uncertainty in that view. DesignArena's Website board measures human preference; its displayed margin-of-error percentage and its explanatory descriptions remain in tension.

OpenAI's system card reports separate raw and length-adjusted HealthBench Professional scores, and MentalHealthBench values at maximum reasoning effort with one standard error across task means. These cohorts are separate from AA's independently evaluated HealthBench. RoboDojo-RC reports partial progress on six selected real-world tasks, and Waddle-1 has a separate multi-model controller and time/call limits. Source-reported costs and times retain their distinct bases; release and observation dates are not evaluation dates.

Waymo's public rider-only dataset still ends in June 2026. The official Design2Code dataset remains a historical screenshot-to-code benchmark; no verified contemporary model results were found. Functional browser completion, screenshot fidelity and human preference remain distinct outcomes.
